# Competitor and Alternative Pages

A content strategy skill for planning accurate competitor comparison pages, alternatives roundups, and head-to-head product evaluations.

## Page types

- Single competitor alternative pages.
- Multi-product alternatives roundups.
- Your product versus a competitor.
- Competitor versus competitor comparisons.

## Capabilities

- Research current features, pricing, limitations, and product fit.
- Build evidence-backed comparison tables and page outlines.
- Organize reusable product facts and source links.
- Align content with search intent without creating thin duplicate pages.
- Recommend audiences and use cases for each product fairly.

## Editorial standards

Use primary sources for factual claims, include verification dates, and distinguish facts from opinion. Do not fabricate features, prices, reviews, testimonials, or customer results. A useful comparison should clearly state where each product is and is not a good fit.
